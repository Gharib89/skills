### Creative video

The sibling of `explainer-video` whose scenes the agent authors freely. Its name is not settled yet. Terms it shares with `explainer-video`, such as excerpt, claim and critic, keep their meaning from [Explainer video](explainer-video.md).

**Chapter**:
A marked run of scenes inside one creative video, two to six minutes long, required once the video runs past about six minutes and allowed at any length. It opens on a hard cut into a scene of the agent's own design, and that scene's first frame carries the chapter's title as the mark a player jumps to. The video keeps one arc across its chapters.
_Avoid_: section (that is an explainer video's), part (that is a separate video), episode, segment

**Chapter plan**:
How a creative video is divided into chapters: each chapter's title, focus, length and the sources it draws on. The agent writes it before the script, and the owner meets it on the storyboard rather than at a stop of its own.
_Avoid_: part plan (that is an explainer video's), outline, table of contents

**Format concept**:
The one idea a creative video is built on, picked before any scene: a game show, a heist, a lab notebook. It owns the arc, the cast, the sound cues and the palette, and each chapter is a step of it.
_Avoid_: theme, style, template

**Format kit**:
The format concept made concrete before the first chapter is authored: its palette, type, cast and their voices, sound cues, and the recurring devices every chapter shares. Changing the kit changes every chapter that uses it.
_Avoid_: style profile (that is an explainer video's), brand, design system

**Staging**:
Anything drawn on screen that makes no claim of its own: a metaphor's props, a sign, a score, a round number, a character. Every on-screen element is staging unless it states a fact as true, such as a figure, a version, a name or a quote; a fact on screen must be one the narration or a cited excerpt states.
_Avoid_: decoration, decorative number, prop

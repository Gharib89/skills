### Creative video

The sibling of `explainer-video` whose scenes the agent authors freely, the `creative-video` skill. Terms it shares with `explainer-video`, such as excerpt, claim and critic, keep their meaning from [Explainer video](explainer-video.md).

**Bed**:
A recorded sound under a creative video's narration, drawn from the format concept's world (a kitchen's room tone, a studio crowd), kept well under the voice and taken out for the hardest step. It is not music unless the concept names a reason.
_Avoid_: score, soundtrack, background music

**Brand**:
A saved, named look a person can give a creative video: its colours, type faces and logo, measured from their own material or from the brand's own pages. It fixes what makes the video recognisable; the format concept still gives each colour its meaning and may add a colour its world needs. A creative video with no brand takes its whole look from its concept.
_Avoid_: style profile (that is an explainer video's), theme, house style

**Cast**:
The roles that speak in one creative video, each with its voice per language and its style line, fixed in the format kit so every chapter hears the same people.
_Avoid_: narrators, voices, characters (a character can be drawn and never speak)

**Chapter**:
A marked run of scenes inside one creative video, two to six minutes long, required once the video runs past about six minutes and allowed at any length. It opens on a hard cut into a scene of the agent's own design, and that scene's first frame carries the chapter's title as the mark a player jumps to. The video keeps one arc across its chapters.
_Avoid_: section (that is an explainer video's), part (that is a separate video), episode, segment

**Chapter plan**:
How a creative video is divided into chapters: each chapter's title, focus, length and the sources it draws on. The agent writes it before the script, and the owner meets it on the storyboard rather than at a stop of its own.
_Avoid_: part plan (that is an explainer video's), outline, table of contents

**Direction**:
One candidate format concept for a creative video, written before any scene with its arc, cast, audience device, sound and colour. A run writes three that differ in kind, not in palette, and marks one recommended; the person picks, or an unattended run takes the recommended one and writes why.
_Avoid_: option, variant, pitch

**Format concept**:
The one idea a creative video is built on, picked before any scene: a world (a heist, a kitchen at service), a format (a game show, a courtroom), or the subject itself drawn directly (an algorithm running). It owns the arc, the cast, the sound cues and the palette, and each chapter is a step of it.
_Avoid_: theme, style, template

**Format kit**:
The format concept made concrete before the first chapter is authored: its palette, type, cast and their voices, sound cues, and the recurring devices every chapter shares. Changing the kit changes every chapter that uses it.
_Avoid_: style profile (that is an explainer video's), brand (a look the kit may wear), design system

**Role**:
One speaking part of a cast: the narrator, a host, an announcer, a learner. Each narration line names its role, and a line that names none is the narrator's.
_Avoid_: speaker, persona, narrator (that is one role)

**Sound palette**:
The effects a creative video uses, one per kind of story event it marks (a wrong answer, a door opening), fixed in the format kit with each sound's source and licence, so the same event sounds the same in every chapter.
_Avoid_: SFX library, sound cues (the kit's whole sound, bed included)

**Staging**:
Anything drawn on screen that makes no claim of its own: a metaphor's props, a sign, a score, a round number, a character. Every on-screen element is staging unless it states a fact as true, such as a figure, a version, a name or a quote; a fact on screen must be one the narration or a cited excerpt states.
_Avoid_: decoration, decorative number, prop

**Stills review**:
A fresh subagent's read of a creative video's contact sheets before the full render, given the sheets and nothing of the plan: what a stranger sees in each frame, the defaults it spots, and notes ranked P0 and P1. It gives no score.
_Avoid_: critic (that is an explainer video's), QA pass

# A simple storyboard for freely authored videos

Prototype for the wayfinder ticket
[A simple storyboard for freely authored videos](https://github.com/Gharib89/skills/issues/980).
Never merged.

## Question

What is the simplest storyboard that lets the owner approve a freely authored
video before an expensive render?

## Prototype

`creative-storyboard/storyboard-prototype.html`, published as a private
artifact page. One example video ("How DNS finds a website", 3 chapters, 14
minutes) on three shapes:

- **A, one page, one stop**: the recommended direction drawn as chapter 1
  stills, the other two as one frame each, one approval.
- **B, words first, then chapter 1**: directions in words, then a scene-card
  board for chapter 1, one approval.
- **C, a board per chapter**: a tab per chapter, in, key and out frames per
  scene, an approval per chapter.

## Verdict (owner, 2026-10-27)

C's layout with one approval. The owner picked C, confirmed that one look
approval covers every chapter, that the chapter plan sits on the board with no
stop of its own, and that three directions with "go with yours" as a one-word
answer fits "as few options as possible". Asked to reconcile C's per-chapter
approval with the one-approval answer, the owner chose C's layout with one
approval: later chapters' boards appear as their subagents finish, open to
comment, and render without waiting unless commented on or the format kit
changes.

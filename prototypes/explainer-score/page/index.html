Score Blind Test

One 30-second cut: a title card, the Kokoro bf_emma narration, an end card. Each lettered clip puts a different score under it, ducked from the same word timings and mastered the same way. Which way of making the score wins as the default?

  1. Play each clip; only one plays at a time. The strip under it shows the scenes (tinted: title and end cards; grey bars: speech). Click the strip to jump.
  2. Listen for: does the score sit under the voice, does it lift on the cards, does it change where the scenes change, does it get tiresome.
  3. Pick one winner. Tick "Acceptable as the default" on every score you would ship, winner included.
  4. Save. The page then reveals how each score was made, its licence and its measured loudness.

Arabic Clone Blind Test

Round 2. You asked for your clone with more energy and fewer pauses, closer to Gemini. Five reads of the same script: your round-1 clone, tuned versions of it, a Gemini read converted to your voice, and Gemini itself as the bar. Names stay hidden until you save.

  1. Play each clip. Only one plays at a time.
  2. Tap the rank chips in order of preference: first tap is 1st. Tap a ranked clip again to clear it.
  3. Tick "Good enough to ship" on every clip you would accept as an Arabic narrator, even if it ranks low.
  4. Tick "Sounds like me" on every clip that you would recognise as your own voice.
  5. Save. The page then reveals each voice, its licence class, render cost and a Whisper error rate.
